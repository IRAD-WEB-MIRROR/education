Press service news

News of the ministry.

2026

  • 30 October 2026
    Autumn holidays 1–8 November

    The first quarter ended on 31 October. Autumn holidays are from 1 to 8 November; the second quarter begins on Monday 9 November.

  • 22 October 2026
    Teaching posts: call for teachers

    Public schools still have vacant teaching posts, most in mathematics, physics, English and the primary grades, and most in the districts. Teachers who take a post in a district school receive a housing allowance. See Vacancies.

  • 5 October 2026
    Lieutenant Governor opens a solar-powered school in Navbahor, Nurzhan

    The 24-classroom school is the twelfth school or kindergarten completed this year. Eighteen more are due before the end of December.

  • 1 September 2026
    The school year has begun

    About 400,000 pupils went back to the public schools of the Zone on 1 September.

  • 30 June 2026
    Graduation Test 2026 results

    The results of the National Graduation Test 2026 are published by the Education Quality Agency on e-Irad, by candidate number and by school.

  • 25 May 2026
    Last bell on 25 May

    The school year ends for grades 1–11 on 25 May. Grade 12 sits the National Graduation Test from 1 June.

  • 2 February 2026
    Classes cancelled in the cold

    Directors may cancel classes when the classrooms are below +14 °C (Order No. 52 of 2012). Pupils of Nurzhan and Talas schools stay at home on 2–4 February; the days are made up in the spring holidays.

2025

  • 1 September 2025
    Kindergarten programme Balbakcha

    From this year state kindergartens work by the programme Balbakcha, prepared by the Research Institute of Pedagogy.

  • 14 March 2025
    1,400 teaching posts vacant

    At the start of the year about 1,400 teaching posts were vacant, most for English and physics in the districts. The housing allowance for teachers who go to district schools is raised from 1 April.

2024

  • 2 September 2024
    New school year: 396,000 pupils

    396,000 pupils began the year in the public schools; 38 schools in Talas and Nurzhan work in two shifts.

2023

  • 20 September 2023
    History of Irad: change to the standard

    The period 1992–2009 moves to grade 7 from the school year 2025–2026, as proposed by the Research Institute of Pedagogy.

2022

  • 15 June 2022
    Dinara Seitkulova heads the curriculum section

    Dinara Seitkulova, a former teacher of history, is appointed head of the curriculum section.

2021

  • 1 September 2021
    Pupils from the guest families

    Children of the families who have residence permits as guests of the Republic (Executive Directive 2021/14) are taken into Talas schools with an Iradi class for newcomers.

2020

  • 1 September 2020
    Schools open again

    After the quarantine all public schools open on 1 September. The first two weeks repeat the fourth quarter of last year.

  • 10 June 2020
    Kanykei Umotova appointed Minister

    Kanykei Umotova, head of the Education Quality Agency since 2012, is the new Minister of Education.

  • 19 March 2020
    Schools closed under the quarantine

    Under Executive Directive 2020/05 all schools are closed from 19 March until further notice. Teachers give tasks through the class teachers; the Research Institute of Pedagogy prepares weekly tasks for grades 1–9.

2017

  • 1 September 2017
    Every subject in Iradi

    The State curriculum standards of 2017 are in force. Every subject can now be taught in Iradi, with the School Terminological Dictionary of the Research Institute of Pedagogy.

2016

  • 1 July 2016
    Toktogul District education office

    With the new Toktogul District the schools of the basin pass from the Lower Naryn education office to the new Toktogul District office in Toktogul town.

2012

  • 20 November 2012
    Rule for cold classrooms

    Order No. 52: classes may be cancelled when classrooms are below +14 °C; the director informs the district office the same morning.

2011

  • 1 September 2011
    Uniform and school pages

    Navy uniform with a white shirt in every public school (Order No. 18). Schools may now open a free page on the Education Network, iradedu.org.

2009

  • 30 June 2009
    Graduation Test: no Russian paper

    From this year the National Graduation Test is set in Iradi and English only. Questions go to the Education Quality Agency.

2007

  • 3 September 2007
    The school year has begun

    Schools in Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl opened for the new year. The ministry continues its work under Executive Directive 2007/09 on the organisation of the administration.

2006

  • 14 December 2006
    Department becomes Ministry

    Under Decree No. 2 of 12 December 2006 the Department of Education is the Ministry of Education. Addresses and telephones stay the same.

2005

  • 25 June 2005
    First National Graduation Test

    Grade 12 pupils sat the first National Graduation Test. Results are sent to the schools by the Education Quality Agency.

2004

  • 1 September 2004
    Teskey-Jar school in a new building

    After the cliff collapse of April the Teskey-Jar school moves into the former farm office. The old building is closed.

  • 1 March 2004
    Our website

    The Department of Education opens its website, education.id.gov, made by the curriculum section with Attelkom. Schools connected under the School Connection Project can write to info@education.id.gov.
