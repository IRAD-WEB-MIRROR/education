Documents and legal acts

Laws, directives and orders on schools.

Executive directive · 26 September 2026 · 2026/31

On school textbooks

in force

At least IS 41 million for textbooks over four years; a printing programme for 2027–2030; expert review of every new title; a code on each copy.

Order of the Minister · 20 August 2026 · No. 41

On the school year 2026–2027

in force

Quarters and holidays: 1 September–31 October, 9 November–26 December, 11 January–19 March, 29 March–28 May; Graduation Test in June.

Full text ›

Order of the Minister · 14 March 2025 · No. 9

On the housing allowance for teachers in district schools

in force

A monthly housing allowance for teachers who take a post in a district school for at least three years; raised from 1 April 2025.

Order of the Minister · 15 September 2023 · No. 27

On changes to the standard for History of Irad

in force

The period 1992–2009 is taught in grade 7 from 2025–2026; grade 8 begins with the 2009 corridor protocol.

Executive directive · 2 August 2021 · 2021/14

On guests of the Republic

in force

Residence permits for the guest families; their children are admitted to public schools.

Executive directive · 18 March 2020 · 2020/05

On the quarantine regime

no longer in force

Schools closed from 19 March to 1 September 2020.

Order of the Minister · 10 April 2017 · No. 7

On the State curriculum standards

in force

Approves the standards for grades 1–12 prepared by the Research Institute of Pedagogy, in force from 1 September 2017.

Order of the Minister · 20 May 2016 · No. 31

On the licensing of private schools and kindergartens

in force

Private schools and kindergartens need a licence of the ministry; the language of teaching is stated in the licence.

Order of the Minister · 20 November 2012 · No. 52

On classes in cold weather

in force

Classes may be cancelled when classrooms are below +14 °C; lessons are shortened to 35 minutes below +17 °C.

Order of the Minister · 16 May 2011 · No. 18

On school uniform

in force

Navy uniform with a white shirt in all public schools from 1 September 2011.

Order of the Minister · 14 February 2011 · No. 12

On the Education Network of the ISEZ

in force

The address iradedu.org; standard pages of schools on Form EN-1; the Education Network Centre answers for the server and the mail.

Executive directive · 6 August 2007 · 2007/09

On the organisation of the administration

in force

Keeps the Ministry of Education among the ministries of the administration.

Decree · 12 December 2006 · No. 2

On the ministries of the Democratic Republic of Irad

no longer in force

The territorial Department of Education becomes the Ministry of Education.

Order of the Secretary · 1 October 2003 · No. 64

On the School Connection Project

no longer in force

With Attelkom: dial-up connection and mail at edu.id.gov for schools, colleges and district education offices.

Act · 1 August 1997 · 1997

Language and Education Reform Act

in force

Twelve grades; teaching in Iradi and English, Russian as an optional subject.
