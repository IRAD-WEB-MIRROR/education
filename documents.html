Laws, directives and orders on schools.

Executive directive · 26 September 2026 · 2026/31

On school textbooks

in force

At least IS 41 million for textbooks over four years; a printing programme for 2027–2030; expert review of every new title; a code on each copy.

Order of the Minister · 16 May 2011 · No. 18

On school uniform

in force

Navy uniform with a white shirt in all public schools from 1 September 2011.

Act · 1 August 1997 · 1997

Language and Education Reform Act

in force

Twelve grades; teaching in Iradi and English, Russian as an optional subject.
