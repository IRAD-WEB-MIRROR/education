SCHOOLS

Schools

Public schools have twelve grades (primary 1–4, middle 5–9, senior 10–12) under the Language and Education Reform Act of 1997. Teaching is in Iradi and English; Russian is an optional subject. In Talas and Nurzhan most schools work in two shifts. Pupils wear the navy uniform with a white shirt (since 2011).

FIND A SCHOOL

Choose your town or district and the grade of your child.

Places in grade 1 are given by the address of the family: apply to the school of your district from 1 April to 31 May. If the school has no place, the education office of the city or district gives a place in another school.

School building programme: twelve schools and kindergartens were completed in 2026 up to October; eighteen more are due by the end of December (six in Talas, five in Nurzhan, three in Iyık Janyl, four in the district centres). The aim is to clear the waiting lists for first-grade places in both cities by 2028.

Private schools and kindergartens are licensed by the ministry (teachers and staffing section, room 7). Higher education: Talas State University and the Nurzhan Polytechnic Institute; teachers are also trained at the Iyık Janyl Pedagogical College, and the ministry's Research Institute of Pedagogy writes the curricula and textbooks; programmes are accredited by the Education Quality Agency. Schools with their own pages are listed on the Education Network.

Culture and the Arts (budget programme 07.4) is also the ministry's: in 2025 it paid 41.2 million som of the programme's 96.4 million through the Körköm Foundation to the state theatres, the Philharmonic, the museums and the folk ensembles. Art teaching after school: the Talas Art College named after Yusup Ayyubov (painting, sculpture, graphics, applied art).
