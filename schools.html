Schools

Public schools have twelve grades (primary 1–4, middle 5–9, senior 10–12) under the Language and Education Reform Act of 1997. Teaching is in Iradi and English; Russian is an optional subject. In Talas and Nurzhan most schools work in two shifts. Pupils wear the navy uniform with a white shirt (since 2011).

School building programme: twelve schools and kindergartens were completed in 2026 up to October; eighteen more are due by the end of December (six in Talas, five in Nurzhan, three in Iyık Janyl, four in the district centres). The aim is to clear the waiting lists for first-grade places in both cities by 2028.

Private schools and kindergartens are licensed by the ministry (teachers and staffing section, room 7). Higher education: Talas State University; teachers are also trained at the Iyık Janyl Pedagogical College, and the ministry's Research Institute of Pedagogy writes the curricula and textbooks; programmes are accredited by the Education Quality Agency. Schools with their own pages are listed on the Education Network.
