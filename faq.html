Questions and answers

Questions of parents and teachers.

Parents

When do I enrol my child for the first grade?

From 1 April to 31 May, at the school of your district, with the birth certificate and the residence record. Children who are six by 1 September may start.

Is there a Russian class?

Public schools teach in Iradi and English; Russian is an optional subject. Licensed private schools may teach in other languages.

The school says there are no textbooks.

Tell the curriculum section (+996-39-2-23-15-20) the school, the class and the book. From 2027 every copy will carry a code.
