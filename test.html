National Graduation Test (Uluttuk Synak)

Pupils finishing grade 12 sit the National Graduation Test in June. It is set and marked by the Education Quality Agency, which works under the Office of the Governor, not under the ministry. A pass is needed for university.

  • Four compulsory subjects: Iradi language and literature, mathematics, history of Irad, English; and one elective.
  • Papers in Iradi or English. Total 500 points; pass mark 36 in each subject.
  • External candidates (without an ISEZ school certificate) register with the Agency.

Results: e-irad.org/test-results, by candidate number and by school.

Questions about marks and appeals go to the Education Quality Agency, not to the ministry.
