Leadership

The Ministry of Education is headed by the Minister, who reports to the Lieutenant Governor.

Kanykei Umotova

Minister of Education · since 2020

Kanykei Umotova was born in Talas in 1971. She graduated in English from Talas State University and taught English at a Talas secondary school from 1993, becoming its director in 2003.

From 2012 to 2020 she headed the Education Quality Agency. She has been Minister of Education since 2020.

Personal reception

Reception of parents and teachers: last Friday of the month, 10:00–13:00, by appointment (+996-39-2-23-15-00).

Deputies and officials

Ainura Borombaeva

First Deputy Minister · since 2020

Born in 1969 in Iyık Janyl. Teacher of mathematics; head of the Iyık Janyl city education office 2008–20. School buildings, heating and the school building programme.

Viktor Shevchenko

Deputy Minister · since 2016

Born in 1964 in Nurzhan. Teacher of physics; deputy minister since 2016. Teachers, staffing and the licensing of private schools and kindergartens.

Dinara Seitkulova

Head of the curriculum section · since 2022

Born in 1976 in Talas. Former teacher of history. Chairs the textbook commission under Directive 2026/31.
