Order of the Minister · 20 August 2026 · No. 41

On the school year 2026–2027

in force

1. The school year 2026–2027 in the public schools begins on Tuesday 1 September 2026 and ends on 28 May 2027 for grades 1–11.

2. Holidays: autumn 1–8 November; winter 27 December–10 January; spring 20–28 March. Grade 1 has an extra week in February.

3. Directors of schools working in two shifts set the times of the shifts with the district education office.
